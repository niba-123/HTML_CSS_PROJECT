About NMSM Government College

NMSM Government College, Kalpetta is a pioneering institution of higher education in Wayanad, Kerala. Established in 1981 under the Government of Kerala, the college initially functioned in a rented building at Kalpetta before moving to its beautiful 25-acre campus at Vellaramkunnu in 1983.

The campus was generously donated by the Neelikkandy Family in memory of Neelikkandy Moidheen Sahib, a visionary leader dedicated to social welfare and education. Located in the heart of Wayanad, the college is the first Government College in the district affiliated with the University of Calicut and has a proud legacy of more than 43 years of academic excellence.

The college offers six undergraduate and four postgraduate programmes in Arts, Science, and Commerce. It provides modern classrooms, laboratories, a well-equipped library, and opportunities in NCC, NSS, sports, arts, and cultural activities. With over 80% female enrolment, the institution is committed to empowering women through quality higher education.

Guided by its vision and mission, NMSM Government College promotes academic excellence, innovation, diversity, and social responsibility while preparing students to become confident, ethical, and self-reliant citizens.
